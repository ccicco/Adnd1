# DMO Gap Report - the book vs the repo

DMO ARC OPENER, ROUND ZERO. The high-level arc names the
Dungeon Master Option: High-Level Campaigns (1995, Skip
Williams, 192 pp.). This report inventories the whole DMO
book against the engine and is, by design, lossless: it
mirrors the book's own structure (the seven maxims, the
adventures chapter, spells and items, item creation,
magical duels, true dweomers, high-level characters, the
dueling appendix, the index), every named section from
the book's own table of contents gets its own line item,
and - per the R566 amended-manifest lesson, applied HERE
AT OPENER rather than by later amendment - every one of
the book's 52 numbered tables gets its own line item
under its chapter (nothing can be skipped silently, no
cyclone-class misses). Every load-bearing prose mechanic
is its own sub-item rather than a clause in a paragraph.
Engine scope unchanged: the rules/ pin headers, the
battery audits, the census. Closure criterion: a box
closes when the feature PLAYS right - compiling is not
the test; a round closes an item by flipping the box in
the SAME commit.

Conventions as the MotP report: [x] verified against the
book text (with the round); [~] verified DIVERGENT; [ ]
open item. Page cites are the PRINTED book pages; the OCR
scan maps printed page N to the session capture (part 1
holds printed 1-90, part 2 printed 91-192, overlap 86-90
resolved to the part 1 text).

CROSS-BOOK SEAM WARNING (standing rule for this arc): the
DMO is a 2e-era supplement that builds on the DMG and the
PHB already pinned, and it references the Complete
Handbooks, Tome of Magic and Legends and Lore (not yet
pinned). NOTHING is auto-credited. A DMO box may only
close on pins that carry the DMO's own text; where the
DMO restates a core rule (saving throw tables, THACO),
the pin carries the DMO's own wording and its own deltas
(the automatic-failure rule, the THACO limits).

ADVISORY-PROSE SCOPE RULING (standing for this arc): the
maxims and the adventures chapter are DM advice, not
game mechanics - they close on doctrine pins (the named
maxim, the demographic ladder, the encounter-planning
doctrine) rather than on data tables. The 52 numbered
tables are data and close on table pins.
