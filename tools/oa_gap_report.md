# OA Gap Report - the book vs the repo

OA ARC OPENER, ROUND ZERO. The new arc names
Oriental Adventures (1986, TSR 1500, ISBN 0-88038-099-3,
David "Zeb" Cook with Gary Gygax and Francois
Marcela-Froideval, 144 printed pages). This report
inventories the whole OA book against the engine and
is, by design, lossless: it mirrors the book's own
structure (character creation, classes and races,
families and caste, honor, money and equipment,
proficiencies, the shukenja and wu jen spell books,
combat and martial arts, events, the terrain encounter
tables, the monster roster, treasure, and the Kara-Tur
chapters), every named section from the book gets its
own line item, and - per the R566 amended-manifest
lesson, applied HERE AT OPENER rather than by later
amendment - every one of the book's numbered tables
gets its own line item under its chapter (Tables 1-86
plus the two DMG-style Orient treasure tables II.A and
III in Chapter 13; nothing can be skipped silently, no
cyclone-class misses). Every load-bearing prose
mechanic is its own sub-item rather than a clause in a
paragraph. Engine scope unchanged: the rules/ pin
headers, the battery audits, the census. Closure
criterion: a box closes when the feature PLAYS right -
compiling is not the test; a round closes an item by
flipping the box in the SAME commit.

Conventions as the DMO report: [x] verified against
the book text (with the round); [~] verified DIVERGENT;
[ ] open item. Page cites are the CAPTURE pages of the
oa.pdf OCR session (315 capture pages, capture p.1 the
front cover through capture p.315 the back cover; this
capture carries no printed page numbers, and it is a
SINGLE capture - the DMO dual-capture rule does not
apply). Contested cells settle book-internal first
(table against prose, list against description), then
against the physical copy. Standing capture quirks
noted at opener: the printed contents page was not
captured (the manifest below mirrors the body structure
itself); heading markup is uneven - the Samurai class
section and the dragon subsections lose their heading
markup in places, and the spell-level subheadings mix
## and ### depths; Table 76 prints a period where the
other tables print colons.

CROSS-BOOK SEAM WARNING (standing rule for this arc):
OA is a 1e-era supplement that builds on the PHB, DMG
and UA already pinned (and it explicitly RETIRES the
European monk in favor of its own). NOTHING is
auto-credited. An OA box may only close on pins that
carry the OA's own text; where the OA restates a core
rule (ability score tables, saving throws, the
Comeliness rules from UA), the pin carries the OA's own
wording and its own deltas. Where the OA monster roster
reprints or references creatures from earlier books,
the OA box closes on the OA's own stat block and
description, not on a credit.

ADVISORY-PROSE SCOPE RULING (standing for this arc):
Chapter 14 (the Kara-Tur overview) and Chapter 15
(daily life) are setting and advice prose - they close
on doctrine pins (the land rosters, the population
ladder, the manners and names doctrine) rather than on
data tables. The 88 numbered tables are data and close
on table pins. The two spell chapters close level by
level (shukenja 1st-7th, wu jen 1st-9th). The monster
chapter closes per monster, the stat block and the
description riding the same round.
