# MM2 Gap Report - the book vs the repo

MM2 ARC OPENER, ROUND ZERO. The new arc names
Monster Manual II (1983, TSR 2021, by Gary Gygax,
156 printed pages, the preface dated October 1982).
This report inventories the whole MM2 book against
the engine and is, by design, lossless: it mirrors
the book's own structure (the preface and
acknowledgements, the how-to-use doctrine with its
number-ranges table, the Monsters from A-Z entries
pp. 8-132, the three Random Encounter Table groups
pp. 133-155, and the Index p. 156). Per the R792
census-correction lesson applied HERE AT OPENER:
the A-Z sub-censuses (the per-letter entry counts
and the family sub-rosters - the daemon, demon,
deva, devil, dinosaur, dragon, giant and
lycanthrope families) are walked AT PIN TIME from
the print, never hand-asserted from OCR; a letter
box closes only when every entry under the letter
is pinned. Every load-bearing prose mechanic is its
own sub-item rather than a clause in a paragraph.
Engine scope unchanged: the rules/ pin headers,
the battery audits, the census. Closure criterion:
a box closes when the feature PLAYS right -
compiling is not the test; a round closes an item
by flipping the box in the SAME commit.

Conventions as the OA report: [x] verified against
the book text (with the round). The opener capture
is the Curtiss PDF upload (capture pp. 1-7, the
front matter, clean text); the archive.org dual
scans are the structural source for the A-Z roster
and the three table groups; contested cells settle
book-internal first, then the physical copy. RITUAL
NOTE (openers): rverify CANNOT restore a NEW gap
report from the pristine sha (the restore deletes
it and the double-run aborts) - openers verify in
working-tree mode (no sha arg); the R564 pattern,
the R614 precedent.
