# RULES

This entry must conform to the rules of Adv. D&D, as published by TSR. No more than five new, non-Adv. D&D, monsters and/or artifacts, total, may be included. Each must be fully explained and described. Each entry must be accompanied by a brief introduction to, or history of itself, of 200 words or less. The entry must consist of a minimum of one 8½ x 11 map, with a maximum of three, and detailed explanatory text. Text must be typed, double-spaced, with one inch minimum margins all around. Each page must be numbered and contain your name. Maps must be drawn in drawing ink — no felt tips or pencil. Entries will be judged on originality, playability and imagery. Professionalism of presentation is a positive factor. Artwork is not mandatory, but salient or unique features should be sketched. Size or bulk will not be considered, nor the lack thereof. Maximum length of copy is as follows, using a three level dungeon as basis: Elite type - 100 pages; Pica - 120 pages. Suggested lengths are 60 and 70, respectively, but could easily be as short as 30 and 40, respectively. All entries become the property of TSR Periodicals. TSR Periodicals assumes no liabilities for said entries. Copyright/waiver form, or facsimile, must accompany entry.
