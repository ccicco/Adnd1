
# AN ADVENTURE...

## IN SEARCH OF THE UNKNOWN

A deserted stronghold deep in the wilderness, rumored to contain great treasures of wealth and magic. . . What a promise of adventure and discovery--as well as danger!! This is the setting for TSR's Dungeon Module B1, "In Search of the Unknown".

Created to be an integral part of the DUNGEONS & DRAGONS® Basic Set (but now available separately as well), this module is the first one in this popular series of releases designed specifically for novice players and Dungeon Masters. The module, fully 32 pages in length, contains a complete adventure scenario for DUNGEONS & DRAGONS® which includes descriptive information as well as legend and background material, optional character lists, monster and treasure listings, and two level maps. In addition to the scenario itself, there are additional wide-ranging sections written especially for beginning Dungeon Masters, who can employ the package as a special instructional module to provide insights and ideas for the construction of their own dungeons, as well as benefit from a discussion of how D & D® is best played and enjoyed. Starting players, too, can profit from their own section offering tips on play.

Experienced players and Dungeon Masters will also find the module worthwhile and useful, and a special section gives advice on easy conversion of the game situation for use with ADVANCED DUNGEONS & DRAGONS®

Like the rest of the releases in this series, this module features a cover folder with interior game maps, as well as a booklet giving all necessary game information and descriptions. The folder comes shrink-wrapped, and the price is $5.49.
