*Hina* takes many forms in many islands. In New Zealand she is both *Hina-Hau-One*, the first woman, and *Hina-nui-te-Po*, the Queen of the Underworld. *Hina* is most closely associated with the Moon. Sometimes she appears as a Goddess in the myths, and sometimes as a Woman. As a Goddess she has the powers of a 15th level Wizard and the above characteristics. As a woman, she has no magical abilities, and has the characteristics of a normal woman.

### **THE HEROES**

The Polynesian Heroes were born in non-human form, and were brought up by their maternal grandparents, from whom they derived their magic. When in human form, they could transform, stretch, or shrink themselves, fly, take giant strides, and perform great feats of strength.
