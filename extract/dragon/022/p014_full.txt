No matter what your answer is, The COMPLEAT STRATEGIST with TWO well-stocked Wargame Headquarters can supply all your needs. Whether you consider yourself a boardgamer, a miniaturist, a fantasy fan, or a military enthusiast - our knowledgeable staff is prepared to assist you.

The COMPLEAT STRATEGIST is the largest store of its kind, both locations stocking games, books, miniatures, and wargaming supplies from all major manufacturers and from many of the smaller companies as well. (If you can't find a particular item on our shelves, we'll do all we can to get it for you!)

If you'd like a copy of our new, free catalog, and to be placed on our mailing list, drop us a note. We'll be happy to send information on what's new and ready for shipment. (We might add that all in-stock merchandise is shipped within 24 hours of receipt of order.)

We also have Gift Certificates available.

Our New York store, located near the Empire State Building, has become a meeting place for war-gamers from all over the world. Both stores are equipped to meet the needs of individuals as well as clubs and organizations for meeting and also for actual gaming. Both stores have gamerooms available (free of charge) so you can watch miniature campaigns or boardgames in progress, or bring your own equipment and you can participate in the fun.

FOR YOUR SHOPPING CONVENIENCE: If you'd like to call, or mail in your order, we accept both Master Charge and BankAmericard (VISA)
