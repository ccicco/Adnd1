If anyone does sell him weapons or anything else that can be dangerous, all the local citizens nearby will do everything in their power to disarm Grunt and arrest the outsider. The seller will be subjected to 4-40 months of hard labor, or a 100-1000GP fine, or both. Grunt can buy armor.
