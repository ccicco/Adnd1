# Dragon Tooth Fantasy Figures

Presents:

The war between the Saurian empire and the Amphibian confederation a spectacular series of the most exciting and original fantasy figures produced in 25 m.m. scale.


Dragontooth Fantasy Figures are designed and packaged to give the fantasy gamer the most for his money. Each of our smaller packages contains at least two dramatic action poses with separate weapons. This gives the creative modeler an opportunity to individualize every figure within a unit, army or diorama. The larger spectacular sets also include a variety of positions and equipment and are noted for the mechanical precision with which the parts are fitted. Dragontooth also includes figures from other well known mythos and fantasies beyond our Saurian and Amphibian range.
