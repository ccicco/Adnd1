Welcome to Fenn Manor, the palatial 19th Century mansion of the esteemed
surgeon Dr. Phineas Fenn - - your host for an intimate late night gathering.
Although you are enjoying Dr. Fenn’s hospitality while passing the late hours in a comfortable overstuffed chair in
the study, most of the other guests seem strangely reserved and aloof, and an air of mystery pervades the atmosphere.
Even the good doctor himself seems unusually remote tonight as the time passes and the hour approaches midnight.
But what could possibly disturb your contentedness as you warm in the glow of your glass of fine imported cognac?
You glance at your watch: it’s only minutes to twelve. Perhaps it's time toa bid your host good-bye, you decide, as you
rise up out of your chair.
Suddenly another quest rushes into the room and grabs your arm with a sense of urgency. ‘‘Come quickly! Doctor
Fenn is dead!!’’ Puzzled and suddenly filled with fear, you rush into the parlor. There are ali the other guests - - and on
the floor, Doctor Fenn’s lifeless body. The other guests eye you suspiciously . . .
Thus the scene is set for the game of SUSPICION - - the adult mystery game for 3 to 6 players. As the ‘‘guests’’, the
players must determine which among them is guilty of the demise of Dr. Fenn (while the guilty player must try to appear
innocent and throw the others off his track). This is done by the players through uncovering clues, assessing evidence,
and clever questioning. Each player tries to fit the pieces together in a test of logic and deductive reasoning (as well as
subterfuge) in order to either identify the murderer while throwing off the other investigators ‘if the player is
investigating) or to avoid suspicion and implicate someone else (if the player is the murderer}. The result is a fascinating
and challenging contest of wiis and logic which will appeal to game players and mystery buffs alike!
SUSPICION is a classic ‘‘whodunit”’ game - - one which can be played over and over again with
engrossing interest. Complex, but without inordinate detail, the game has all the elements to
fascinate a broad range of players who can easily become caught up in its unique appeal.
SUSPICION is a complete boxed game featuring a hard-backed playing board in full color and
including all the necessary game materials: playing pieces, evidence envelopes, clue cards, notes
pad, and dice.
