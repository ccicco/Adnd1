The fantastic and multi-faceted nature of warfare on the world of Tekumel comes alive through
TSR’s LEGIONS OF THE PETAL THRONE!
Designed as a companion volume to M.A.R. Barker’s remarkable game EMPIRE OF THE PETAL
THRONE, this 44-page booklet contains all the necessary details to create mass battles with miniature
figures featuring the myriad human and nonhuman forces of Tekumel.
LEGIONS OF THE PETAL THRONE includes a wealth of material for the ever-increasing number
of gamers who have discovered the unique fascination of this strange world. In addition to a set of
basic rules, there is a large section on magic, with the bulk of the booklet detailing the advanced rules
for the Qadardali or Great War. And in two extensive appendices by M.A.R. Barker, additional
valuable information is included on the armies of the various empires of Tekumel and the details of
their many unit and army formations. Quite simply, a must for anyone interested in the fantastic
world of the Petal Throne...
