# GAMMA WORLD

## A New List of "Treasures" To Be Found

by Gary Gygax

It is indeed very nice to get a list of 100 instant treasures for use when a group is poking around in an area which is likely to contain artifacts from the past civilization, but 100 items are not enough. It also seemed apparent to me that some useful and/or common things were omitted from the items contained in the GAMMA WORLD rules book, so I sat down and devised an 80 "treasure" list which is to be used to supplement the existing one — and I urge you to do yet a third list of your own to use with the two provided, a 66 roll determining which of the lists will be used to find each treasure discovered.
