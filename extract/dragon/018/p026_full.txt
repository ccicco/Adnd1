Armor will have some bearing on the outcome of an encounter. Here are the simple adjustments we use, towards saving throws:

Plate +2

Chainmail +1

Leather 0

No armor -2

Armor has no effect on the poisons that have been taken internally. If a portion of a potion is drunk, each individual portion will have it's own saving throw. Plus the amounts are applicable to each other, for their own separate saving throw.
