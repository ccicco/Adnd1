

# ORIGINS!

Including Penn Con XV
June 22, 23, and 24, 1979
Widener College, Chester, Pa.

Sponsored by Strategy & Fantasy World

## Fifth Annual National Wargaming Convention

Enjoy Tournaments and Demonstrations in Board Gaming, Miniatures and Role Playing Games. Compete with the top players from across the country. Seminars, Workshops, and Meetings with the outstanding game designers, authors, manufacturers and hobby leaders from around the world. All major manufacturers will be represented...the latest in games, rules and miniatures will be show cased at Origins '79.

Multi-media Hobby Center...awards...displays...open gaming...auctions and much, much more will happen throughout the weekend.

Featuring the Third Annual WRG Ancients Competition and DipCon '79.

Accommodations and restaurants for every budget located nearby...Free transportation to all gaming centers and events.

This is one convention you can't afford to miss!
