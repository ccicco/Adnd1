# BIRTH TABLES AND SOCIAL STATUS
Empire of the Petal Throne

by G. Arthur Rahman

TSR's Empire of the Petal Throne is undoubtedly one of the finest and most detailed role-playing games in print. But its very richness challenges players to add extra dimensions to those points of special interest to them. In our area of the country, EPT players like to have fully fleshed-out backgrounds for player and non-player characters alike. The standard rules of EPT start each character off at the same social level. But is life really like that? Doesn't the favorite son of a great nobleman have opportunities for training and education far surpassing those of a laborer's offspring? With this in mind, I present the following Birth Tables for the Empire of the Petal Throne.
