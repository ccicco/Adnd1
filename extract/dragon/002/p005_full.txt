Others;

1) Rest(R): must be used once each set after the first.
2) Jump Back (JB): moves user 1 leg length back. Can kick after it if opponent hasn't moved and they were arm's length before jumping.
3) Jump Forward (JF): moves user 1 leg length forward. Can only strike if this brings opponents within arms length.

4) Duck (D): brings body mostly below high and middle strikes and kicks.

Remember, the advancement combat is not designed to kill, but it can. Most combats are finally settled by concession.

Summary — you can kick and block once or twice, block 1, 2, or 3 times, one strike & one block, or use two strikes.
