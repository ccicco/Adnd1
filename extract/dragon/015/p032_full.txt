# SPECIAL EVENT! DUNGEON! CHAMPIONSHIP

Friday 18 August 1978

Over $750 value in prizes!

Up to 256 players 8 players - 8 character games

3 rounds -- initial, semi-final, final -- 3 rounds - 1 day

All winners of the initial round will be awarded $5 gift
certificates

Semi-finalist winners will be awarded $15 gift
certificates

Co-sponsored by Grenadier & TSR

This event will be pre-registered, $2 entry fee

There will be a huge D&D tourney DMed under Gary
Gygax and Bob Blake and special D&D events for
beginners DMed by J. Eric Holmes. We will also
feature AFRICAN CAMPAIGN and RUSSIAN
CAMPAIGN tournaments judged by their designer
John Edwards. Of course Saturday will be opened by
the traditional Dawn Patrol game of FIGHT IN THE
SKIES. All types of boardgames will be played from
battle simulations to fantasy and science fiction. Also
dozens of dealer exhibits including miniatures of all
kinds will be a main attraction. See you there!
