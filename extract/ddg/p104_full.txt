Appearing as a giant black spider, this deity is known for its delight in re-
ceiving human sacrifices. Its powers include the ability to summon 30
phase spiders per day, the power to make all arachnids obey, and the
ability to transfer the powers of a spider to its most powerful high priest.

Its evil clerics work diligently to keep their god satisfied by way of human
sacrifices. It rewards them with a gift of a pet large spider once per each
ten sacrifices.

Tyaa’s cult was banished from Lankhmar, but its worshipers can still be
found in the mountains of Nehwon. Only women are permitted in the high
priesthood and Tyaa demands the sacrifice of body parts from her more at-
tractive worshipers. The Falconers of Tyaa are the male arm of the cult and
always have some type of bird as a fighting companion. They, like the

SPIDER, SALT priestesses, are able to communicate with all avian life.
