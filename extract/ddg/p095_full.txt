This creature appears as a huge humanoid with green skin and turquoise
hair and beard.

As a ruler of the water elementals, Straasha has double the power of nor-
mal water elementals, plus the ability to cast all water spells at the 20th
level of magic-use. He is the only one of his type not affected by heat-
based attacks. He can summon 3-18 of his kindred to aid him in battle.

Vampire trees save vs. fire at a -2 penalty, and take +1 extra point of
damage for every die of damage done by flame. They fear fire, and will
never send their leaves near flame.

Straasha can strike with two waves of water per round that hit for 8-48
points each; these are created by the being out of thin air.

While he has few (if any) human worshipers, many water elementals and
water creatures are aware of Straasha and pay him homage.
