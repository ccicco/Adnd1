club named Dwarfcrusher that hits for 4-40 points of damage, and he
usually wears several belts of woven dwarfbeards.

that its roots mingle with the roots of every other plant in the world. On a
more mundane level, Rillifane can appear on the Prime Material Plane as
a green-skinned elf clad in bark armor and carrying a magic bow. Arrows
shot from this bow by Rillifane always slay their target if they hit (no
saving throw).
