Raiko, favored of the gods, is a fighter of enchanted monsters, and for his
many victories over giants, demons, the undead, and other ghastly things
he has been given greater endurance (thus his increased hit points) and
has achieved a speed of movement that is quite unhuman (thus his greater
dexterity). His weapons are the longbow and sword; with his arrows he
can achieve twice the distance and damage of normal longbowmen, and
his sword is said to be swifter than the striking serpent. Raiko is known to
some as Yorimitsu.
