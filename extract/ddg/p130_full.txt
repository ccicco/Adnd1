The Plane of Shadow co-exists with the Prime Material Plane, and is a
result of the interaction of that plane and the Positive and Negative Ma-
terial Planes. The Negative plane provides the darkness, and the Positive
plane the light; these meet at the Prime Material Plane, which casts the
shadow. Thus the Plane of Shadow is a place of distorted and mutable
shadow-creatures, of white, black and all shades of gray.
