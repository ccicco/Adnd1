MASTER TYPES

These beings live in other dimensions called half-worlds (demi-planes). In
these “worlds” every animal of the earth has a ruler which is the most
perfect specimen of its type. They are all dangerous if dealt with from a
position of weakness. It seems that every species is aware of the need for
some sort of deity; therefore in the cases of dogs (for example), all dogs
worship their own image, which in turn becomes Roofdrak, ““Master of all
Dogs”.

Elric has a conscience of a sort, and sometimes tries to do the ““honorable
thing”’, but he is responsible for much evil also. He often rationalizes that
the end justifies the means. He is very arrogant towards most humans and
extremely vengeful, and though he views the power of stealing souls
through Stormbringer with great distaste, he does it anyway to survive.
ARROWS OF LAW

Elric and his race are familiar with the other planes of existence and have
traveled them in the distant past to visit gods in their home planes. Such
knowledge has made this race the most powerful magic-users of the Prime
Material Plane. It also gives Elric a large advantage in that he can call on
forces of great power to aid him in dangerous situations.

There are 20 arrows of law in a quiver, each of which will always hit and
kill one chaotic creature (save vs. death applicable) at normal longbow
ranges.
