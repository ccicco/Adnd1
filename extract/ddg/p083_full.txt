Hachiman has the following abilities: he shape changes at will, is not af-
fected by any spell of the 7th level or less, and is never affected by any

The god has a throwing knife that turns into an ancient red dragon when
cast in anger at an immortal. If the dragon dies in battle, it returns to
dagger form, otherwise being forced to change back into dagger form for
a 48 hour period after fighting up to 100 melee rounds.

In hand-to-hand combat, he uses his two-handed sword which only he can
lift. This is a + 3 weapon that acts as a sword of sharpness and also does 4-
