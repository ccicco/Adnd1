The DDG print p. 131 FIG. 4 CHARACTER ALIGNMENT GRAPH - the D&DG walk
round thirty, the pixel round. Both text voices (the extract/ddg
tesseract pass and the archive.org 1st-print djvu) read near-zero on
this page - line art; the evidence is the pdftoppm renders (300/600/
1200 dpi, committed c8bf507 and 1028ebe) read by the vision passes,
collated against the tesseract TSV label geometry, with the TARTARUS
eyeball confirmation by Curtiss.

The graph: a circle divided by CURVED lines converging at the center
(crescent regions, not crossing Venn lenses), the small center circle
reading NEUTRAL (the true neutral), the eight wedge labels printed as
stacked two-line caps. The four curved descriptors ride the four
corner alignments: Saintly arcs the LAWFUL GOOD region, Beatific the
CHAOTIC GOOD, Diabolic the LAWFUL EVIL, Demoniac the CHAOTIC EVIL;
the axis wedges and the center carry none.

The nine alignments, the print reading order (the wedge labels
top-to-bottom then left-to-right, the center circle last), with the
compass position and the descriptor ordinal (0 = none):

0. NEUTRAL GOOD - north, no descriptor
1. LAWFUL GOOD - northwest, Saintly
2. CHAOTIC GOOD - northeast, Beatific
3. LAWFUL NEUTRAL - west, no descriptor
4. CHAOTIC NEUTRAL - east, no descriptor
5. LAWFUL EVIL - southwest, Diabolic
6. NEUTRAL EVIL - south, no descriptor
7. CHAOTIC EVIL - southeast, Demoniac
8. NEUTRAL (the center circle) - center, no descriptor

The tesseract TSV anchors the readable labels (NEUTRAL GOOD 202, LAW-
FUL GOOD 463, CHAOTIC GOOD 464, LAWFUL NEUTRAL 853, CHAOTIC
NEUTRAL 854, LAWFUL 1193, CHAOTIC 1193, EVIL 1241 at the 300-dpi
page); the NEUTRAL EVIL label and the center circle are vision-only.
