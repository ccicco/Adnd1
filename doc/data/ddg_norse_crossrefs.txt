DDG Norse mythos cross references (print pp. 113-124, the 2nd
printing). The companion-block pointers, the family and rivalry cross
references, the plane parentheticals and the worship pointers of the
section.

Odin three companion blocks (print p. 114)
  Hugin and Munin (Thought and Memory) - aerial reconnaissance,
    verbal messages; Odin sees and hears through them
  Sleipner - the eight-legged steed; only Odin rides (or Odin-ordered
    one-ride trips for beings in extreme need)
  Freke and Gere - the plane-walking punisher wolves of Odin

Frey household (print p. 117)
  Blodug-Hofi - the fire-resistant horse, defends the fallen Frey
  Gullin-Bursti - the golden boar, pulls the war chariot; a dwarf gift
  Skirnir - the mortal shield-man, auto-teleports to Alfheim a split
    second before a death blow

Thor chariot goats (print p. 122)
  Tanngrisner (white) and Tanngjost (black) - eaten by Thor and
    friends, reborn from bones and skin in morning light

Family and marriage cross references
  Odin and Frigga - husband and wife (she spins flax into gold)
  Balder - father of Forseti (the hall Glitner of gold and silver)
  Bragi - husband of Idun (the Apples of Youth)
  Frey and Freya - Vanir twins, children of Njord and Skadi
  Thor - father of Modi and Magni; Sif is Thor wife
  Loki - blood brother of Odin; offspring by the giantess
    Angur-boda: the Fenris Wolf, Jormungandr and Hel
  Vidar - a son of Odin
  Tyr - lost his right hand to the Fenris Wolf at the binding
    (the dwarf-forged cord), the contract that made him the law god
  The Norns (Urd, Verdandi, Skuld) - past, present and future;
    they tend Yggdrasil and advise the gods

Rivalries and enmities (the mlb Elenoin-Grahuk rivalry precedent)
  Heimdall vs Loki - the sworn-enemy pair (Loki mocks the
    guardianship of Bifrost)
  Odin vs Loki - the Ragnarok pairing (good against evil at the
    last battle; evil triumphs, both hosts destroyed)
  Aegir vs the Aesir and Vanir - fights trespassers in his sea
    realm; friendly and sociable but watched
  Garm vs the heroes and gods - killed several who came to
    retrieve a loved one from Hel

Plane parentheticals
  Odin   - Gladsheim (Valhalla)
  Surtur - Gladsheim (Jotunheim)
  Thrym  - Gladsheim (Jotunheim)
  Loki   - Pandemonium or Gladsheim (by his current status with
    the gods)

Giants and Jotunheim pointers of the intro
  frost giants -> Thrym (leader and deity)
  fire giants  -> Surtur (leader and deity)
  Jotunheim - attached to the planes of Gladsheim, the anomalous
    blot of evil; the giants have clerical or illusionist powers
    and magical weapons

Handmaiden pointers
  The Valkyries - Freya is their leader; they also serve as Odin
    hall maids and Frigga handmaidens (Fulla, Vara, Lofn, Vor,
    Gna, Eira, Hlin, Syn, Vjofn, Snotra); they ride pegasi, the
    offspring of Sleipner
  The Einheriar - the honored slain of Valhalla (always 4th level
    and above), the army of the Norse gods at Ragnarok
