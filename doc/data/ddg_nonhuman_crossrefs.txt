DDG Nonhumans' Deities cross references (print pp. 105-112,
the 2nd printing). The NOTE list of beings treated as lesser gods
though they very rarely have human worshipers, plus the section's
walk-forward worship pointers (all detailed in the MONSTER MANUAL
or FIEND FOLIO except Lolth, whose stat block rides this section).

MONSTER MANUAL lesser gods (the NOTE list)
  Demon:   Demogorgon, Juiblex, Orcus, Yeenoghu
  Devil:   Asmodeus, Baalzebul, Dispater, Geryon
  Dragon:  Bahamut, Tiamat

FIEND FOLIO lesser gods (the NOTE list)
  Demon:   Lolth (detailed in this volume)
  Elemental Princes of Evil (unnamed here)
  Slaad:   Ssendam, Ygorl
